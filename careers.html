Careers · Lusaka, Zambia

Build the farm that runs itself.

We map land in 3D, read the crop from the air, find water underground and design farms to fit the terrain. The long-term goal is autonomous agriculture for Africa, built here in Zambia. We are a small team and every early hire shapes how the company works.

See open roles Send an open application

How we work

  • The field is the truth.Everyone spends time on farms. A model that doesn’t match the ground is wrong, however good it looks.
  • Rigour over decoration.Statistics, physics and agronomy first. We show our working and say how sure we are.
  • Software of the highest quality.There’s no reason agricultural software in Africa shouldn’t be the best anywhere. We build it that way.
  • Own the outcome.We’re paid for results on the farm, not for reports. You see a job through from survey to harvest.

Process

How we hire.

Short, practical and respectful of your time. We tell you where you stand at every step.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Send the form below with a CV or portfolio link. A few honest lines on why beat a long cover letter.

  2. 02

    Conversation

    A 30-minute call about your experience and what you want from your next role.

  3. 03

    Practical task

    A short task close to the real work, such as a field plan, a dataset or a small feature. We discuss it together.

  4. 04

    Offer

    Meet the team, ideally on a farm. A decision within a week of the final conversation.
