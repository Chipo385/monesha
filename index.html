Services

What we do

  1. 3D Land Mapping

    Available

    A drone flies planned lines and photographs the whole property. We turn the pictures into a 3D model with contours, slopes, drainage, boundaries and areas.

  2. Crop Monitoring

    Available

    Regular checks through the season show how each part of a field is growing, what has changed and where to look first.

    What you get
    • See stress early. Vigour maps flag water stress, nutrient shortage, pests and disease, often before they are obvious from the ground.
    • Count and compare. Stand counts, gaps, orchard tree counts and field-by-field change over time.
    • Act on it. Every flag is checked in the field, then turned into a scouting list, a yield estimate or a map of where to put fertiliser and lime.

  3. Groundwater Survey & Mapping

    Available

    Where the water is likely to be and how deep: drilling targets, wet and dry season levels, dams and catchments.

    Look underground

  4. Farm Design & Engineering

    Available

    Fields, roads, drainage and the complete irrigation set-up, from borehole and pump to pivot, drip or sprinkler, designed on your real terrain.

    See the irrigation set-up

  5. Precision Spraying

    Coming soon

    Treatment only on the patches that need it, with less chemical and no wheel damage to the crop.

  6. Watch

    Available

    Satellite checks, scheduled flights and field visits, with alerts for flood, fire, pests, disease, wind and change.

    See the dashboard demo

Method

From untouched land to a running operation, in four moves.

Scroll through the same land as it is photographed, modelled, designed and watched.

01 · Survey
  1. 01

    Survey

    A drone flies planned lines and takes overlapping pictures of every part of the land. The frames left on the ground are photographs being taken. Soil samples and a groundwater survey add what a picture cannot see.

    Output: site baseline & soil map

    3D Land Mapping
  2. 02

    Model

    A 3D digital twin of surface and subsurface: topography, drainage, groundwater, and a ranked recommendation of what the land should grow given soil, climate and your goals. Here the twin strips away the canopy to the bare ground, shows where water gathers, and marks where boreholes should go.

    Output: digital twin & crop plan

    Look underground
  3. 03

    Engineer

    The farm is designed on the surveyed ground: fields on the contour and the irrigation set-up drawn in full. Green is the borehole supply, blue the mainline to the pivots, orange the submain to the orchard.

    Output: master plan & build drawings

    See the pivot design
  4. 04

    Watch

    Continuous satellite monitoring for flood, fire, pests, disease, wind and change, on an online platform, with physical assessments every two to three weeks for commercial estates.

    Output: alerts, prescriptions, field reports

    See the alerts

About
