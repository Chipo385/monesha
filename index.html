01 · Method

From untouched land to a running operation, in four moves.

Each step produces data the next step depends on. Nothing is designed until the ground has been measured, and nothing is built until it has been modelled.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Centimetre-level 3D mapping of the land, crop-health imaging from the air, geo-referenced soil sampling with laboratory analysis, and a groundwater survey of the site.

    Output: site baseline & soil map

  2. 02

    Model

    A 3D digital twin of surface and subsurface: topography, drainage, groundwater, and a ranked recommendation of what the land should grow given soil, climate and your goals.

    Output: digital twin & crop plan

  3. 03

    Engineer

    The farm designed inside the twin: parcels on the contour, boreholes on the fracture, irrigation sized to the hydraulics, drawn to engineering detail and built with our field engineers.

    Output: master plan & build drawings

  4. 04

    Watch

    Continuous satellite monitoring for flood, fire, pests, disease, wind and change, on an online platform, with physical assessments every two to three weeks for commercial estates.

    Output: alerts, prescriptions, field reports

02 · Groundwater

Drill where the water moves, not where it is hoped for.

On Zambia’s crystalline basement, groundwater rarely sits in a flat layer. It collects in the weathered saprolite and moves through fractures and shear zones in the rock below. A borehole that intersects a water-bearing fracture can yield many times more than one drilled a few metres away.

We combine terrain analysis, lineament mapping and ground geophysics to model the weathering profile, the seasonal water table and the structures that carry water, then site boreholes on that evidence.

  • Weathering profile and depth to fresh rock
  • Wet- and dry-season water table surfaces
  • Fracture and shear-zone targets for drilling
  • Surface water, dam catchment and storage capacity

03 · Crop fit

Soybean, oranges or timber? Let the land answer.

Every candidate crop is scored against what the survey measured: rainfall and its reliability, soil reaction and drainage, slope, frost exposure and the water that can be pumped sustainably. The owner’s horizon matters too: an annual crop pays this season, a macadamia orchard in year five, a plantation in year ten.

Move the site conditions and watch the ranking change. In a real engagement the inputs come from laboratory results, the groundwater model and decades of climate records for the exact location.

    04 · Engineering

    Infrastructure designed on the terrain it has to work on.

    A centre pivot crossing a swale gains pressure and over-waters the low ground. Coming over a crest it loses head and starves the crop. Mainlines laid against the contour erode. We design inside the twin so these problems are solved on screen, not discovered in the field.

    Irrigation hydraulics
    Pipe sizing, pressure regulation along the span, pump duty and energy, application uniformity
    Water infrastructure
    Borehole manifolds, storage, dam and canal layout, solar pumping
    Land layout
    Contour parcels, terraces, drainage, roads, firebreaks and riparian buffers
    Deliverables
    Contractor-ready drawings, CAD and GIS files, machine task files for guidance and variable-rate application

    Our forward-deployed agricultural engineers stay with the project from survey to commissioning, so the design that was modelled is the system that gets built.

    After the build, the farm is never unobserved.
