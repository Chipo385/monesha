# Brief — what matters for this project

Distilled from the Antigravity conversation "Moneshe Precision Technologies Landing Page"
(Jul–Nov 2026) and the Monesha business plan. Follow these rules when changing the site.

## Company

- Name: **Monesha Precision Tech** (Ltd). Not "Moneshe". HQ: Lusaka, Zambia.
- *Monesha* means to look deeply, look further, or deep vision, in Bemba. Use it in brand copy and the logo story.
- Corridors: Mkushi, Chisamba, Mazabuka, Mpongwe.
- A product-and-service company on a forward-deployed model: field teams plus a software core.
- Long-term goal: autonomous farms. Present it as direction, not as a current capability.

## Scope (current)

- Plants only: field crops, orchards and fruit plantations, agroforestry and timber.
- **No livestock or poultry** for now.
- Capabilities to show: soil testing and sensors, topography and 3D terrain, surface and
  underground water (borehole siting), crop suitability ("soybean, oranges or timber?"),
  3D digital twin, irrigation and infrastructure planning to engineering detail, and monitoring
  for flood, fire, pests, disease, wind and change. Satellite plus field visits every 2–3
  weeks for commercial estates.
- Next phase: in-house spray operations, equipment installation, forward-deployed
  agricultural engineers.

## Confidentiality

- **Never name equipment, sensors, drones, compute hardware, software vendors or data
  providers.** The tech stack is a trade secret. Talk about results, resolutions,
  turnaround and capabilities only.
- **Long-term plans** beyond agriculture live in `docs/BUSINESS.md` only (kept out of git). Never mention them on the website or in client-facing material.
- Don't publish pricing on the site. (The business plan has ZMW price ranges for proposals.)

## Content rules

- No emojis.
- No locations outside Zambia in company copy unless the owner approves them.
- The software platform is not named publicly for now (internal name in docs/BUSINESS.md).
- Precise, scientific wording, but readable by a farm owner or bank.
- No invented testimonials or case results. Demo visuals are labelled "Illustrative",
  "Example" or "Concept render".
- Currency: Zambian Kwacha (K / ZMW).
- 3D terrain must look like real land (asymmetric, eroded, river valleys), not symmetrical
  sine waves.

## Visual direction

- All farm visuals on the site are rendered from the same digital twin, so the site looks consistent from top to bottom. Avoid stock or AI photos.
- Dark, editorial, technical. References the owner liked: motionsites.ai (halden-stories,
  ai-philosophy, dot-portrait) and striated monoline logos (MTS, Record, serro, warp).
- Accent: lime `#c6f36b` on charcoal `#0b0d0c`. Type: Inter Tight, Instrument Serif
  italic for emphasis, JetBrains Mono for labels.
- Logo: the depth aperture: rings receding into the land (looking deeply) ending in a lime point (what we find). Files: `assets/logo/h-depth-aperture*.svg`. Owner prefers the dot over a cross or arrow.
